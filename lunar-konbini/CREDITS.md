## Scene assets
The 3D scene uses CC0 / public-domain assets; full provenance and per-file hashes are in the project manifest:

- Poly Haven (CC0): PBR textures (hinoki planks, painted plaster, rubber tiles, metal plates, linen, lunar dust/rock, etc.), plant/prop glTF models, reflection HDRI.
- ambientCG (CC0): Plastic013A, PaintedMetal004/012, PaintedMetal shutter textures.
- NASA Blue Marble Earth (public domain), via the reachable three.js examples CDN mirror.
- All packaging/signage icons are generated locally (abstract icons, no text/logos).

See ../assets/MANIFEST.md and ../docs/ASSETS.md for the full licence record.
